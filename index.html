    Online booking

    Share one link. Players ask for a court from their phone, with no app and no account.

    1. Choose a time. One court, or several courts and hours in one request.
    2. Choose how to pay. Pay at the venue, or send by GCash and enter the reference number.
    3. Staff approve it. The request shows on the schedule and the slot is held. Nothing is confirmed until staff approve.

    Screenshots of the actual booking page, with sample data.

    What is in the system

    It is modular and customizable. Turn on only the modules you need, then set prices, levels, schedules and access to suit your venue.

    Sales tracking. Menu, packages, discounts, shifts and order summaries.

    Open Play. Player list read from your Reclub page, payments, check-in and vouchers.

    Queuing and TV display. Checked-in players from your Reclub list, with levels, groups and automatic calls.

    Court bookings. Schedule, weekly blocks and events.

    Online booking page. Single or multiple courts, with a payment reference.

    Accounts and membership. Balances, credit limits and member prices.

    Inventory. Stock, restocks, suppliers and item cost.

    Expenses and coaches. Bills, recurring bills and payouts.

    Ledger and analytics. Accounts, reimbursements, reports and Excel files.

    Future plans

    Planned and not yet available. Plans can change.

    Payroll and attendance. Time in and out, pay and coach payouts in one run.

    Automatic online payment. GCash, Maya and cards that confirm on their own.

    Multi-branch. One owner login for several venues, with combined reports.

    Inventory and suppliers. Purchase orders, barcode scanning and reorder alerts.

    Loyalty points. Points players earn and use at your venue.

    DUPR-ready queuing. Matches based on DUPR ratings, with scores prepared for DUPR.

    Notifications. Your match is near, your booking is confirmed, or a balance is due.
